Tamagotchi P1

A native C rewrite of the Tamagotchi P1. To play, choose your ROM dump (tama.b, 12288 bytes). It stays in your browser only and is never uploaded anywhere.

Keyboard: ← ↓ → or A S D · F = 10x. The game saves by itself in this browser; while the tab is closed the pet's time stands still.
